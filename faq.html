Have questions about our organic stewardship, delivery logistics, or CSA memberships? Explore our detailed knowledge base below or search directly.

1. Organic Certification & Farm Operations

Yes. Every square foot of Agrovia's 2,400-acre agricultural campus is certified under USDA National Organic Program (NOP) regulations and verified by California Certified Organic Farmers (CCOF). We maintain strict zero-tolerance policies against synthetic fertilizers, chemical insecticides, sewage sludge, ionizing radiation, and GMO cultivars.

We deploy biological integrated pest management (IPM). This includes propagating native insectary hedgerows of sweet alyssum and yarrow to harbor parasitic wasps, releasing predatory lacewings and ladybugs, and utilizing organic kaolin clay sprays to protect fruit skins.

2. CSA Box Subscriptions & Doorstep Delivery

Subscribers on our Family Farmstead and Commercial tiers can customize up to 3 produce items every week using our member harvest portal before Sunday midnight cut-offs. Taster plan boxes are pre-curated based on peak field harvest flavor.

You have total flexibility to pause or skip your deliveries anytime with 48 hours notice. You can also choose to have your vacation box donated to our regional food bank partners.

3. Wholesale Supply & Agronomic Consulting

Standard wholesale minimums begin at 250 lbs per order for mixed vegetables or 1 full pallet for root vegetables and orchard fruit. Scheduled weekly restaurant route minimums start at $200 per drop.
